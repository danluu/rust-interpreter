# Reviewed public cache archival

Four exact completed Nushell type-relations caches are reviewed and unapplied: native, check, baseline and candidate. The qualified helper verified ownership, terminal corpus membership, original paired artifacts, complete byte/metadata inventories and closed files. The review covers 112,833 paths and 13,455,643,977 unique bytes; each target and inventory hash is in inventory-review.json.

Apply only the recorded batch hash after this review is checked in. Preserve all historical records and artifacts; no private, active or unrelated cache is selected. Archive readback and terminal retirement verification remain required.
