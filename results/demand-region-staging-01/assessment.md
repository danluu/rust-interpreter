# Independent region staging matches the adopted native words

All 348 bytecode library controls pass in debug and release, with 12 ignored
observers per profile. Three new controls cover wide/duplicate branches, Calls,
assertion and arithmetic/memory fault tails, 1,024-operation splits, unsupported
successors, invalid fragment selections and code-limit refusal without publication.

The block capture reconstructs 1,050 ordinary functions and 60 scalar bodies, then independently stages 20,033 regions and resolves 23,863 declared edges in private vectors. Every reassembled word, original map span, assertion identity and entry agrees.

The exhaustive capture reconstructs 1,245 ordinary functions and 69 scalar bodies, then independently stages 23,406 regions and resolves 27,611 declared edges in private vectors. Every reassembled word, original map span, assertion identity and entry agrees.

Setup takes 68.18 seconds across four commands. No original
project guest reruns and no fragment code is published; ordinary native unit
controls still execute their original synthetic programs. Closure binds exact
sources, capture bytes, test logs and compact reconstruction receipts.

regions and creates dense temporary metadata for every fragment. Those costs
must not enter the measured demand engine. Next record per-region boundaries
