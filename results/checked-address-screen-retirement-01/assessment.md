# Retire completed, owned screen intermediates

The two completed scalar-Copy and scalar-Copy/budget token screens will not
be repeated. Under the shared benchmark lock, their eight custom namespaces
and two native targets lost only eligible compiler intermediates. The driver
validated terminal owner/command receipts, full-campaign rejection where
applicable, exact source pins and paths, open-file ownership and per-file
identity immediately before removal.

All1,888 protected file hashes remain unchanged, including executable and
bytecode evidence. The6,294 removed files had3,824,413,816 logical bytes;
observed free space rose from12,119,007,232 to15,271,505,920 bytes. Logical
removed bytes do not equal physical APFS reclamation, and other workloads can
change free space concurrently. No private or other-session cache was touched.
This one-shot cleanup is not a performance experiment or a replacement for
the independent disk monitor. Raw inventory, plan and protected-file receipts
remain local at the paths identified in the summary.
