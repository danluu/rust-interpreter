# Directory-mode fixture qualification

All ten bounded fixtures passed once, and the complete actual child, raw output, twelve frozen inputs, launcher, detached supervisor and terminal were independently verified. The fixtures exercised nested permission changes and removal, refusal of changed/foreign entries, durable completion evidence after postcondition failure, and uncertainty after publication failure.

Only tiny task-owned temporary trees were changed. No real compiler prefix was chmoded or deleted, and no compiler, provider or nested process was launched. The canonical 16/9/8 GiB admission and fixed 120-second/60-CPU-second, 256 KiB per-file and 2 MiB retained-output bounds remained unchanged.

`experiments/old-compiler-directory-modes-01` contains exact tested helper and fixture source. The complete twelve-input copies and their original path/stamp/hash catalog are retained here; actual scope and observation limitations remain in `independent-verification.json`.
