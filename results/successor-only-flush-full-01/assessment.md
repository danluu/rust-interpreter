The fresh three-cycle primary comparison completes all 154 commands and rejects
adoption. Across 15 valid edits, median paired candidate/baseline ratios are
0.96750 wall and 0.95377 CPU: observed improvements of 3.25% and 4.62%. The
full protocol's A/A envelopes are 7.32% wall and 4.48% CPU. Wall plus its margin
is 1.04073, so the required incremental wall gate fails. CPU plus its margin
is 0.99858. Candidate/native wall remains 1.64382; candidate/anchor is 0.76292.

The earlier 40-command screen passed, with a similar 3.34% observed wall gain
and a smaller 2.09% A/A envelope. The full result supersedes that screen for
adoption. Keep both results and their original gates. This does not establish
that the mechanism has zero benefit; it fails the required full evidence rule.
Do not rerun it unchanged or select a quieter subset of its observations.

Original assertions, deliberate wrong edits, native/custom outcomes, within-state
candidate/control bytecode and catalogs, and final source restoration all pass.
The controller stops before folded, pgrust, private rg-aot and Nushell. Their
572 commands remain unstarted, as does the separate full-parser qualification.

The final serialized audit verifies 1,584 distinct frozen inputs, all source
pins/restoration and 122 retained artifacts. The successful terminal receipt
records the controller's deliberate rejected verdict. The 525 workspace tests
per profile, 119 strict/cache checks, three exact profiles, 13 selected/prepared

Keep successor-only flushing as a possible composition component. Before another
runtime candidate, use the saved exact native operation maps to separate memory
address/checking work from actual small Copy/Load transfers. Their coarse spans
remain a much larger part of the captured workload than branch operations.
Any new component needs its own safety proof and cost evidence; the failed full
gate is not grounds to remove checks or relax measurement requirements.
