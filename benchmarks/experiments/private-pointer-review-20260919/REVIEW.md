# Park the overlapping pointer draft before building

The September 19 draft was prepared before checking the complete experiment
archive. Its core raw-pointer mechanism already exists on
`experiment/private-pointer-promotion-20260912`. That experiment qualified 344
Rust tests per profile and 16 typed fixture commands, then improved paired
complete-command wall and CPU by only 0.34% on its fixed five-edit token screen.
All 32 command outcomes passed; the preregistered 10% wall target failed.
See [the original assessment](../../../results/private-pointer-screen-token-01/assessment.md).

Stop the overlapping direction. No build, fixture, new guest execution or timing
was launched for this draft. The old plan and production files were restored to
their committed contents. The patch, extra source and draft plan here preserve
the unfinished work as unqualified material, not an implementation to adopt.

The draft also admitted references, unlike the original raw-pointer candidate.
No measured evidence justifies that additional scope. Its naive union of old
and new candidates could exceed the transform's 256-slot bound and discard
previous numeric promotions; the historical plan explicitly prevented this.
These are further reasons not to treat the draft as a qualified continuation.

Before another implementation, search experiment plans AND result assessments,
then tie a distinct mechanism to current native samples. The historical screen
does not prove all pointer optimizations worthless on every later runtime, but
it supplies no basis for repeating this one unchanged.
