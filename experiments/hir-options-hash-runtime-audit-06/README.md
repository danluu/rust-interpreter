# Preflight05 saved-evidence audit draft

This is an unrun, preflight-only successor to audit05. Its four Python files
adapt the saved audit to `runtime-preflight-retry-05/routes.json` and its new
owner. The descriptor is pinned to
`893d6102741b5206a829e2f5e130614667140b1eacc09d0c55616f7311f82e6a`.
No runtime packet, compiler probe, manifest preparation or audit has run through
this directory. The retry suite has 33 source-derived tests; its actual proof
bindings remain `None`, which the executor refuses before creating evidence or
starting a child. A separately retained ordinary development run passed those
33 cases; it is not the frozen bounded qualification.

The original audit05 `reader.py`, `audit_io.py` and their tests stay at their
actual53-qualified paths. The recipe, preflight and final-runtime predicates
stay in audit04 with their separate actual45 proof. The source04 factory and
runtime prerequisite reader retain actual52 ownership, and the startup policy
and original owner/tests retain actual39 ownership. None of those files is
copied or relabeled. The new owner, entry, controller, descriptor, preparer and
two launch wrappers require the separate retry controls and `test_retry.py`.
The immutable Reader's route-specific `phase_paths` and `owner` functions are
not used for the new attempt.

`audit.py` retains the full prerequisite, historical-copy, retirement, catalog,
gzip EOF, snapshot-selection, child-history and output-membership checks. Its
new owner callback receives independently reconstructed actual39 and retry
proofs. The failed preflight04 is authenticated against the published failure
review, all original packet/retained-record references, and the exact
`plan.failed_predecessor` field. It remains a failed zero-child predecessor;
it is not added as a successful catalog owner. No removed historical-copy path
is admitted to a physical read API.

`bootstrap.py` authenticates the original helper proofs and the separate retry
proof before importing their sources, expands the original compact packet,
and constructs the existing inspection union. Original prepared headers and
logical snapshot inputs stay unchanged. The manifest has exactly eight keys:
`policy`, `files`, `actual53`, `phase45`, `preparation`,
`preparation_launcher`, `startup_controls`, and `retry_controls`. Its policy is
`runtime05-retry-saved-audit-source-manifest-v1`. The descriptor is an explicit
source row and a plan reference, not an extra manifest header.

`prepare_manifest.py` requires the complete actual53/45/39/retry source, raw and
closure chains plus the completed producer preparation's seven files, original
invocation and startup source manifest. It does not recursively duplicate the
prepared provider table. The proposed reviewed source inventory is the four
new files, the four original audit05 helper/test files, the eight retry
source/test files, and the original `environment.py`: 17 distinct routes, with
policy `runtime06-reviewed-sources-v1`. A final inventory must bind their
reviewed bytes after all source and control bindings are complete.

The phase order is producer preparation, actual preflight and observed closure,
supplemental manifest preparation, then independent saved audit. Manifest
preparation may follow producer closure before the runtime phase; the audit
itself requires all seven actual phase digests. No phase digest is invented or
filled from a future output. Fresh audit-owned paths are:

- `ROOT/.work/runtime06-saved-audit-preflight-manifest-01`
- `ROOT/.work/runtime06-saved-audit-preflight-manifest-preparation-execution-01`
- `ROOT/.work/runtime06-saved-audit-preflight-execution-01`

The report path remains the descriptor's exact
`R/.work/hir-options-hash-runtime-preflight-independent-verification-05.json`.
`execute.py prepare` takes the actual producer preparation and wrapper digests;
`execute.py audit` takes the manifest/manifest-preparation closure and seven
actual phase digests. Both modes require the reviewed inventory path and SHA.
These are source contracts, not prepared invocations.

Existing limits are unchanged: 16 GiB entry, 9 GiB live stop, 8 GiB floor,
600-second canonical wait, 900 CPU seconds, 1200-second read bound,
1250-second observer bound, 4 MiB report/file limit, and at most 256
supplemental rows totaling 8 MiB. The executor retains the original corrected
post-Popen wait/closure body, records observation failures, and uses no signals,
retry, provider probes, network or compiler calls. The final manifest must fit
these limits; no larger cap is implied by this draft.

The new runtime preflight's 16/9/8 admission policy is distinct from this
read-only audit's same numerical bounds. It is a proportional policy for the
two metadata probes, not a measured RSS or swap peak. Installation remains
outside this successor and retains its existing 24 GiB entry requirement.
