# B308 standalone retention

This proposal preserves the completed 19-child B3 assembly/auxiliary-strip history, all 335-output catalogs, every compressed source/proof snapshot, original source08 and its proposal, the rejected source07 proposal, seven compression-helper controls, and all three independent verifier attempts. The two verifier failures remain failures: SDK alias inventory selection, then `otool -L` versus `LC_LOAD_DYLINKER` projection. The final verifier passed without repeating any assembly, compiler, provider or loader workload.

The controller rechecks every selected exact input and directory membership before and after archiving, replays the saved 19-child associations and qualification records, and fully expands all 220 inner gzip proof blobs through EOF/CRC with their exact logical hashes. The outer archive preserves all 287 logical proof mappings, uses backward content deduplication, verifies every logical member and consumes the complete outer gzip through EOF/CRC. No extraction or subprocess is used by the archive helper.

The finite caps are 2,048 members, 64 MiB per file, 512 MiB logical input, 384 MiB unique physical payload, 192 MiB compressed output, and 416 MiB expanded tar bytes. These explicitly account for both 57 MB freezes and both original/successor proposal catalogs; they do not alter any compiler or candidate evidence cap. Entry requires 9 GiB plus the 416 MiB archive reservation, canonical exclusion waits at most 600 seconds, and live free space must remain at least 9 GiB. The archive WORK is `.work/beta-composition08-retention-01`, outside the candidate monitor's evidence prefixes, so it cannot silently add a candidate stage to the already frozen native proposal.

All completed B308 WORK is included, including its two small strip fixture objects. Live B3, compiler, SDK and registry payloads are excluded; their frozen catalogs and actual guarded provenance remain. The prior producer-control archive is an immutable hash-bound reference. This does not qualify native-stock controls, the hash driver, applications or performance. Read-only preparation and actual archive launch are separate; the archive remains unrun until exact packet approval.
